# Open Creative Director

## Purpose

Choose the strongest form for the current short drama without turning one successful structure into a universal template. Creative freedom means every formal choice is available; it does not mean choices are arbitrary.

## Establish the decision context

Infer what can be safely inferred. Resolve only the dimensions that change the work:

- target platform, region, language, and viewing situation;
- requested artifact: idea, script, prompt, shot package, video, or publishing package;
- audience familiarity: what they already recognize, feel, fear, desire, mock, or argue about;
- intended emotional promise and aftereffect;
- current model and production constraints only if execution is in scope;
- evidence freshness when “latest,” traffic, or platform fit matters.

Do not turn this into a generic intake form, but do not write a full script from a thin brief either. When the brief lacks two or more of platform, form or genre, length, and an existing idea, run the intake in SKILL.md 「剧本接单两态」 in one message, and no full script until the owner picks or says "直接写". 数量按 SKILL.md 「剧本接单两态」与 [script-intake-contract.md](script-intake-contract.md) 执行，此处不复述数字；校验器 `scripts/validate_script_gate.py` 是最终判定。

## Use collective recognition as a doorway

The first layer should be legible from an existing human schema: a familiar relationship, social ritual, status gesture, object, rule, fear, wish, or contradiction. Borrow recognition, not a copied plot.

An unfamiliar world can still open through a familiar action: hiding evidence from a parent, refusing a seat, rehearsing an apology, dividing an inheritance, covering for a colleague, or breaking a small promise. Explain only what changes the current action.

Avoid two opposite failures:

- **alien novelty:** new lore, terminology, and rules arrive before the viewer has a human foothold;
- **recognition without invention:** a known trope is reproduced with new names and cosmetic styling.

The aim is familiar access plus an unfamiliar consequence, perspective, image, rhythm, or moral angle.

For a grounded near-future world, keep ordinary apartments, cafés, and streets familiar, then alter only the few technologies that change the story's choices or consequences. Generic neon and wall-to-wall devices are not substitutes for world rules.

## Pick or invent the dramatic engine

No engine is mandatory. Examples include:

- escalating cause and consequence;
- a relationship micro-shift;
- a real-time deadline;
- a dilemma with no clean winner;
- withheld information with fair visible clues;
- observational slice-of-life;
- visual or verbal pattern and break;
- parallel or conflicting points of view;
- a loop whose meaning changes;
- a social ritual going slightly wrong;
- atmospheric threat or slow recognition;
- absurd premise played with sincere behavior;
- a performance, confession, task, or object that reveals character;
- a purely visual gag or transformation.

This list is a palette, not a taxonomy. Combine, discard, or invent forms as the material requires. A story does not need public humiliation, a villain, a reversal, a moral debate, or a cliffhanger unless those choices earn their place. It does need a nameable want, an opposing force, a visible losable stake, escalation, and an earned turn or progression. Form is free; tension is not.

A visual motif should grow from the dramatic proposition, then recur across wardrobe, accessories, material, or shape only where each repetition changes how the audience reads the character's pressure. Repeated decoration without that change is a style sticker, not a motif.

When a comedy mechanism comes from a reference, strip away named IP, people, songs, quoted lines, brands and the original staging. If the playable pattern no longer works after de-shelling, it is imitation rather than a reusable mechanism; rebuild or reject it instead of copying a category list.

### 喜剧机制算子

需要原创喜剧时，用下面这条算子生成机制，而不是挑选一个熟悉的笑点类别。

先选一个可被利用、误读或困住人的现象——媒介语法、不可见状态、场所规则、道具行为、视角差或信息差——把它写成一句世界规则。再给角色一个具体欲望和明确的知情位置，让他据此制定第一次策略；欲望与知情差必须独立于原案例，换掉专名后仍然成立。

之后重复同一模式，但每一轮只改变一个决定性变量，并写出这一轮的可见代价：信息暴露、关系损失或身体后果。轮数只取到观众能识别规则并读出升级为止。升级的定义是处境改变、损失扩大、后果更难逆转，不是骂得更狠或再加一个反派。

收束依据机制与人物欲望选择：规则反咬、意义翻转、人物自证或让观众重新理解前面的重复，都只是可选结果，不是固定结尾菜单。

最后做去壳检查：删掉具名 IP、艺人、歌曲、原台词、舞台与品牌后，这条戏是否仍可拍。仍成立才是机制；只能靠原作识别的，是借壳，重做或放弃。

### 赛博微综艺与规则真人秀机制算子

当选题方向进入「AI 微综艺 / 规则真人秀 / 极端挑战」时，按以下六步机制算子推进，而不是生套剧情短剧结构：

1. **认知借壳（心智外壳）**：优先借用大众已有稳定预期的经典真人秀/综艺框架（如身份逆差交换/改造、极限生存淘汰/MrBeast流、职场整顿观察、荒诞相亲修罗场、反向职业体验），首秒建立情境与预期，省去世界观冷启动成本。
2. **超现实规则引擎（物理自由）**：以严肃纪实、专业主持解说语态，包装现实中无法实拍或成本极高的超现实大场面（全校粉笔战争、游戏满级大招大乱斗、末日废土职业体验、脑洞具象化道具战），制造严肃形式与荒诞内容的极致反差。
3. **去伦理化的人物极化**：利用虚拟角色“零真人伦理伤害”红利，设立极致反差人设（如网络烂梗王 vs 暴躁拳王、傲慢资本家 vs 赛博流浪汉），释放观众真实吐槽欲与代入感，消除传统真人秀的道德侵犯与舆论公关危机。
4. **轮次递进与可见代价**：单期 4–6 分钟浓缩 2–3 轮规则博弈，每轮只改变一个决定性变量（时限收紧、筹码翻倍、淘汰名额减半、惩罚加码），并在画面中呈现不可逆代价（当场淘汰、道具损毁、公开处刑）。
5. **去壳原创与合规检查**：剥离原综艺的具名商标、台词与具体明星姓名（如不直接叫《变形计》或冒用真实艺人），转为独立原创机制 IP，避免侵权维权与平台封禁风险。
6. **视听与机位契约**：明确规划综艺专属镜头语言——Confessional 小黑屋单人采访机位（内心独白/吐槽）、GoPro/手持晃动跟拍第一视角、主持演播室全景解说与抓马花字、弹幕互动与原生商单植入槽位。

If there is no twist, something else must progress: knowledge, pressure, intimacy, dread, comic pattern, sensory fascination, or the viewer's interpretation. Whatever progresses must make the want harder or more expensive, or the viewer's reading must change; repetition is not progression.

## Earn the next moment

For short feeds, inspect the first three seconds as the earliest continuation test. Do not force a single hook grammar. The reason to stay may be:

- an action already in motion;
- social recognition (“I know this feeling”);
- an unanswered visual relation;
- a comic mismatch;
- beauty, scale, rhythm, or material fascination;
- a credible threat or deadline;
- an odd behavior that reveals a private rule;
- an immediately useful or emotionally precise observation.

The opening must honestly belong to the work that follows. Do not manufacture a disconnected shock, false promise, or title.

## Build people at the required resolution

A character may be admirable, flawed, idealized, opaque, soft, cruel, passive, capable, ordinary, or contradictory. Choose the resolution needed for this duration and form.

Prefer observable specificity over biography:

- what the person notices first;
- what they do while avoiding an answer;
- what they protect in public;
- whose presence changes their voice;
- what small action betrays a larger value;
- what they misunderstand about another person;
- what a choice costs immediately.

Do not add a trauma, wound, quirk, or “flaw” merely to satisfy a character template. Do not make every character equally eloquent or self-aware.

## Use playable task and scene event conditionally

When a scene is organized by character action, two thin directing concepts can make it executable:

- `acting_task`: what the character is trying to do to a person, object or space **right now**, expressed as an actable verb plus the current tactic;
- `scene_event`: what occurs that changes available action, knowledge, relation, goal, physical state or perception.

`wants` is the state the character hopes to reach; `acting_task` is the present attempt. “She wants respect” is not playable; “she keeps the host from ending the meal by slowly redistributing every plate” is. A task can change tactics after an event without changing the larger want.

Write event causally when the form needs causality:

```text
story_state_in → scene_event → changed possibilities → story_state_out
```

These fields are form-sensitive and optional. An observational film's event may be the viewer finally reading a spatial relation; a single-shot visual gag may use a physical rule break; a purely formal transformation may need no character task. Do not invent trauma, villains, reversals or “one major event per scene” to fill a schema; but every form must still say who wants what, what opposes it, and what can be lost. Conflict means a losable stake, not shouting.

## Let structure emerge from pressure

Choose duration, scene count, beat density, reversal frequency, and ending shape from the minimum time required for the intended experience to read. Platform limits constrain delivery; they do not dictate a fixed story skeleton.

Check whether each unit changes at least one meaningful variable: information, power, goal, emotion, physical state, relation, expectation, or sensory pattern. A quiet pause may be the change if it lets the viewer recognize what the character cannot say.

## Autonomous commitment

Internally compare genuinely different directions, not three cosmetic variants of one trope. Reject options that are:

- dependent on copied IP expression;
- instantly predictable from the genre label;
- reliant on exposition rather than behavior;
- unfilmable within the requested production scope;
- trend-shaped but emotionally empty;
- likely to lose platform eligibility for no creative gain.

Commit to one direction once the brief is sufficient or the owner has picked a pitch. When the owner brings their own idea, first run the tension-gate diagnosis quoting their text, offer two or three upgrade options per failing row, and rewrite only after they choose.

例外：大纲与分集请求（SKILL.md「故事·大纲·连续分集」入口）在同一条回复内交付全部集数大纲，诊断与推荐修改放在大纲之前，不得以「你定完我再写」推到下一轮；「等用户选定才动笔」只约束剧本正文。

## Anti-template and originality checks

Use judgment, not a numeric score:

- **swap test:** if names, jobs, and props can be replaced without changing the piece, it is too generic;
- **prediction test:** if the viewer can state every beat from the premise, add a real perspective or consequence, not random information;
- **voice test:** if all speakers could exchange lines, restore distinct immediate aims and social position;
- **residue test:** preserve an awkward pause, mixed motive, imperfect response, or concrete aftereffect that a tidy content machine would erase;
- **promise test:** title, first readable moment, body, and ending belong to the same emotional contract;
- **production test:** when visuals are requested, the decisive action can be shown with available assets, space, time, and model behavior.
- **stake test:** name who is carrying the pressure and what they can lose; if nothing can be lost by anyone on screen, the piece has texture but no tension.
- **model-default test:** the writing model has its own favourite names and premises, and every skill on the same model inherits them. Before delivering, search every character, company and title name and ask whether, renamed, this is the most common story of its kind. Blind runs on `claude-opus-5-5` (2026-10) kept producing: 小满; heroines surnamed 沈 or 许, given names with 禾 or 砚 (such as 周砚), and the recurring 顾承泽 and 国栋; and premises such as divorce cooling-off plus account reckoning (half of all topic cards involved 账), the housewife who is secretly a legendary lawyer, rebirth foresight, appraisal or pawnshop windfalls, the chauffeur who overhears the back seat, and an auction house's chief appraiser. This is not a ban list. If the owner picks one of these, keep it and differentiate it on purpose; otherwise rename and change the setup.
- **on-screen test (premise and power):** the edge that drives the story — a power, a secret, an information gap — must be visible on someone else's face or in a public action. Foresight held in the hero's head, floating text, inner monologue, or reciting law articles and ledgers lose on screen. A power with a cost is a strength, but the rule must not make the hero so passive that each payoff needs a detour.

Avoid self-conscious “anti-AI” decoration. Messiness without causality is still artificial.

## 输入具体度与可生成改写

- 核心冲突写成「谁 vs 谁 + 具体前史」，不用题材标签代替对抗关系。
- 大纲、开场等环节分别生成可替换模块，再按共同真源串联；不让一条提示词同时包办全部环节。
- 改稿卡住时可先换能同时承担节奏、动机与结尾的核心物件，不以新增人物或随机反转遮蔽机制问题。
- 开生成前按已知模型短板重新调度场景；改一拍通常比在大量 take 中硬追不可生成动作更便宜。
- 双时间线需要时分别锁互斥的调色、曝光、介质与运镜语言；这是有成本的可选结构合同，不是双线作品的默认模板。

## Hand off to this Skill's Seedance production base

Once the creative choice is coherent, translate only the execution-relevant facts into this Skill's Seedance production system:

```text
viewer experience / chosen form / current scene truth
→ character, place, prop, and state assets only where needed
→ decision-critical shots or a single-shot contract
→ first/end states and movement
→ provider-neutral prompt
→ live adapter and real-pixel QC
```

Do not rebuild a large production package for a script-only or single-shot task.
